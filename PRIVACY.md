# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024

## Introduction

LifeMate ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our mobile application.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

LifeMate is designed as an **offline-first** application. All your data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to:

1. **Backup to Google Drive** - When you enable backup, your data is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before being uploaded to your personal Google Drive account
2. **Export Data** - When you export data to CSV/PDF, you choose where to save or share the files

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
- All data is stored locally on your device using Isar database
- Data includes: weight entries, finance records, medications, reminders, and notes
- This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly backup or export it

### Cloud Backup (Optional)
- If you choose to backup to Google Drive:
  - Your data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before upload
  - Only you can access your backups (stored in Google Drive AppData folder)
  - We do not have access to your backup data
  - You can delete backups at any time

## Third-Party Services

### Google Drive
- **Purpose**: Cloud backup (optional)
- **Data**: Encrypted backups of your app data
- **Privacy**: Your backups are stored in your Google Drive AppData folder, which is private to your account
- **Google Privacy Policy**: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Google Fonts
- **Purpose**: Typography
- **Data**: None (fonts are downloaded locally)
- **Privacy**: No user data is transmitted

## Permissions

LifeMate requests the following permissions:

### Android
- **Internet**: Required for Google Drive backup (optional feature)
- **Storage**: Required for exporting files
- **Notifications**: Required for reminder notifications

## Data Security

- **Encryption**: All backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM
- **Local Storage**: Data is stored securely on your device
- **No Network Calls**: The app does not make any network calls except for optional Google Drive backup
- **No Analytics**: We do not use any analytics or tracking services

## Data Deletion

- **Delete App Data**: You can delete all app data from Settings > Clear All Data
- **Delete Backups**: You can delete backups from Settings > Backup & Restore
- **Uninstall App**: Uninstalling the app removes all local data

## Children's Privacy

LifeMate is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

## Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access**: View all your data within the app
- **Export**: Export your data to CSV/PDF
- **Delete**: Delete all your data at any time
- **Backup**: Create encrypted backups to Google Drive
- **Restore**: Restore from backups
